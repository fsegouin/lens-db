Sigma 85 mm ƒ/1.4 DG DN Art

Last verified 12 Aug 2026 · 4 revisions · 37 sales in 90 days · Cite this page
Sony E Leica L Full frame Prime In production

The Sigma 85 mm ƒ/1.4 DG DN Art is a full-frame short-telephoto prime introduced in 2020 for the Sony E and Leica L mounts. It replaced the 2016 DG HSM design for mirrorless bodies at little more than half its weight, and remains Sigma's fastest 85 for those mounts.
